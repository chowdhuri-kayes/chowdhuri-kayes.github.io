05 / Applied thinking

How I approach operational challenges.

Illustrative case scenarios, not client results or audited performance claims.

RECONCILIATION

Investigating payment discrepancies

Challenge: An expected claim payment differs from a remittance record.

Approach: Match claim references, service periods, units and payment records before identifying the variance.

Review the proposed workflow

Confirm the payer's reason, check for duplicate or adjusted claims, document unresolved items and route corrections to authorized staff. Validate the next remittance before closing.

EVV & PAYROLL

Resolving incomplete visits

Challenge: A fictional visit has a missing clock-out and an inconsistent timesheet.

Approach: Review approved evidence, verify actual service duration and record the exception before processing.

Review the proposed workflow

Confirm applicable rules, use the approved correction process, escalate unverifiable visits, and reconcile payroll and billing separately.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Designing safer administrative support

Challenge: A team wants AI assistance for document organization.

Approach: Begin with invented records, define permitted outputs, assess data destinations and require human review.

Review the proposed workflow

Verify approved agreements and features before real-data use. Test with synthetic inputs, limit access, and keep consequential approvals with qualified people.
